# LC3 Module 02 — Market demand: source-backed CSV collection

This package consolidates checked factual evidence. **No figures are generated yet.**

- `lc3_documented_use_and_interest.csv`: field demonstration and research evidence; research interest is not demand.
- `lc3_california_field_trial_2025.csv`: eight documented pours, six with LC3 or LC3+slag, two Type IL controls. No LC3 tonnage was reported.
- `lc3_market_context.csv`: national cement market context, not LC3-specific purchases.
- `usgs_us_cement_shipments_2019_2025.csv`: existing USGS all-cement annual shipment data (includes exports), with estimated years marked. Not LC3 demand.
- `lc3_projected_market_signals.csv`: prospective manufacturing/market announcements, not observed demand.
- `aceee_lc3_readiness_condition_definitions_2026.csv`: eight published conditions and scoring system, **not** 50-state scores.
- `data_availability.csv`: transparent inventory of what is and is not quantitatively available.

Primary sources: https://www.aceee.org/accelerating-adoption-limestone-calcined-clay-cement/ ; https://www.aceee.org/resource/i2401/ ; https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/cement-statistics-and-information ; https://nrmcc.com/lc3-demonstration
