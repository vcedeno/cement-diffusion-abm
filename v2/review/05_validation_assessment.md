# Technical Review 05: Calibration and Validation

## Purpose

Evaluate the calibration strategy, predictive validation,
geographic performance, and temporal separation of inputs
in the existing v2 model.

This review uses the implementation and stored results.
Reported metrics have not been independently reproduced.

No model modifications are proposed.

## 1. Calibration and validation design

The model separates two estimation stages.

### Manufacturer initiation

A firm-level hazard model is estimated using historical
manufacturer observations through December 2022.

### Market diffusion

RMX purchasing and product-class parameters are calibrated
against observed cement market shares through December 2022.

Historical manufacturer start dates are replayed during
market-layer calibration.

During predictive validation, manufacturer starts are
generated by the estimated hazard model.

Classification: Methodological strength.

This design separates manufacturer initiation errors from
market-layer parameter estimation.

## 3. Finding V01: Calibration objective

Classification: Modeling choice requiring evaluation.

The calibration objective combines:

- National monthly mean squared error, 2010-2022.
- Demand-weighted annual state error, 2020-2022.

The state component has a coefficient of 0.01.

The calibration therefore emphasizes national diffusion.

### Candidate recommendation

Evaluate state-level residuals before changing the
calibration objective.

Investigate whether geographic errors arise from
supplier coverage, plant rollout, purchasing behavior,
product-class restrictions, or imports.

Do not introduce additional calibration flexibility
solely to improve aggregate fit.

## 4. Finding V02: State-level discrepancies

Classification: Documented predictive limitation.

The stored 2025 validation results include:

State          Model    Observed
Delaware       0.828    0.189
New Mexico     0.880    0.438
Pennsylvania   0.724    0.391
New Hampshire  0.709    0.309
California     0.005    0.096
Nevada         0.007    0.122
Texas          0.342    0.474

These discrepancies are larger than the
national-level error.

### Candidate recommendation

Prioritize geographic diagnostics using the
largest state-level residuals.

Examine the relevant input evidence before
attributing discrepancies to a particular mechanism.

## 5. Finding V03: Evaluation-period demand

Classification: Validation interpretation issue.

The model constructs 2025 RMX demand by scaling
2024 demand using observed state-level USGS
shipment ratios from 2025.

Therefore, 2025 model evaluation is conditional
on observed demand conditions.

This does not establish that 2025 PLC shares
were used in parameter estimation.

However, it differs from a fully prospective
forecast using only pre-2023 information.

### Candidate recommendation

Clarify the validation description.

Distinguish prediction of PLC diffusion from
the use of observed evaluation-period demand.

Consider a supplementary diagnostic using demand
projected only from pre-validation information.

## 6. Finding V04: Manufacturer-date uncertainty

Classification: Methodological strength.

The validation procedure distinguishes confirmed
manufacturer initiation dates from documentary
upper bounds.

For upper-bound dates, earlier simulated starts
are not penalized as timing errors.

This avoids treating uncertain evidence as exact.

### Candidate recommendation

Preserve this distinction.

Report manufacturer-level timing and false-start
results alongside aggregate initiation counts.

## 7. Finding V05: Observed PLC proxy

Classification: Measurement limitation.

The historical PLC target is approximated using
USGS blended cement shipment share net of its
2016-2019 baseline.

This is a proxy for PLC diffusion rather than
a direct PLC-only shipment series.

### Candidate recommendation

Clearly describe this measurement assumption.

Consider its implications when interpreting
state-level calibration and validation errors.

## 8. Consolidated assessment

Strengths:

- Separate manufacturer and market estimation.
- Explicit held-out evaluation period.
- Manufacturer initiation predicted during validation.
- Recognition of uncertain manufacturer dates.
- Reporting of both national and state-level metrics.

Issues requiring investigation:

- Nationally dominated calibration objective.
- Substantial state-level discrepancies.
- Evaluation-period demand used as an input.
- Measurement uncertainty in the PLC proxy.

The reported national performance is encouraging,
but geographic predictive performance warrants
additional investigation.
